Basic science rotation

Lab Bootcamp

Two timelines from the basic science rotation, one per wet-lab technique track — both run on the same cell line.

Both experiments ran on the same Rat2 fibroblast cell line, after a shared first session on lab safety and core bench skills. From there the two tracks diverged: Labs 2–6 built an RT-qPCR experiment testing whether ascorbate changes Collagen I expression, and Labs 7–9 built a western blot comparing single-protein against total-protein normalization.

RNA & RT-qPCR Labs 2–6

  1. Lab 2 Isolation

    Cell Culture & RNA Isolation

    Subcultured Rat2 fibroblasts and isolated RNA from ascorbate-treated and control cells, to get RNA clean enough to reliably measure gene expression from.

  2. Lab 3 Synthesis

    Reverse Transcription & RNA Integrity

    Removed trace genomic DNA and reverse-transcribed the RNA into cDNA, checking its integrity first so a degraded sample wouldn't undermine the rest of the experiment.

  3. Lab 4 Specificity

    Primer Validation I — Gradient PCR

    Ran a gradient PCR to find the annealing temperature at which each primer produced one specific product, since a non-specific primer would make later results uninterpretable.

  4. Lab 5 Efficiency

    Primer Validation II — Specificity & Efficiency

    Confirmed specificity on a gel, then built a dilution series to calculate primer efficiency — only primers between 90–110% efficiency are valid for accurate quantification.

  5. Lab 6 Expression

    RT-qPCR Experiment

    Ran the full qPCR experiment testing whether ascorbate treatment changes Collagen I expression — the question the whole track was built to answer.

Western Blot Labs 7–9

  1. Lab 7 Quantification

    Protein Isolation & BCA Assay

    Isolated total protein from cell lysates and measured its concentration by BCA assay, to set up a fair comparison between normalization methods.

  2. Lab 8 Separation

    SDS-PAGE & Membrane Transfer

    Separated the proteins by size on a stain-free gel and transferred them to a membrane, using a technique that keeps total lane protein measurable alongside the reference bands.

  3. Lab 9 Detection

    Immunoblotting & Imaging

    Probed the membrane for reference proteins and imaged the result, comparing a single reference protein against total lane protein as the loading control.

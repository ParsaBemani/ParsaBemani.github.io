Basic science rotation

Lab Bootcamp

A flowchart of the basic science rotation — two wet-lab technique tracks, run in parallel on the same cell line.

Every session ran on the same Rat2 fibroblast cell line, building toward two experiments — one asking whether ascorbate changes Collagen I expression by RT-qPCR, the other comparing single-protein against total-protein normalization by western blot.

Basic science rotation

Lab Bootcamp & Journal Club

The two halves of the same rotation: hands-on technique training at the bench, and the weekly paper discussions that ran beside it.

Click a stop to open that session — one stop per track at a time.

Western Blot Track

Goal: compare single reference-protein normalization against total-protein normalization for western blots.

Western Blot · Stop 1

Protein Isolation & Quantification

Lysed cultured RAT2 fibroblast cells to extract total protein, then ran a BCA assay with a BSA standard curve to determine each sample's protein concentration.

Skills gained

Cell lysis BCA protein assay Standard curve calculation
Western Blot · Stop 2

SDS-PAGE & Membrane Transfer

Cast and ran polyacrylamide gels to separate proteins by molecular weight, then transferred the separated proteins onto a PVDF membrane via semi-dry transfer.

Skills gained

Gel casting SDS-PAGE Protein transfer technique
Western Blot · Stop 3

Immunoblotting & Imaging

Probed the membrane with primary and secondary antibodies to detect reference proteins (β-actin, GAPDH), imaged the blot, and compared normalization strategies.

Skills gained

Immunoblotting Antibody probing Densitometric image analysis

RT-qPCR Track

Goal: test whether ascorbate treatment changes Collagen I expression.

RT-qPCR · Stop 1

Cell Culture & RNA Isolation

Practiced aseptic technique subculturing RAT2 fibroblasts, then isolated RNA via spin-column purification and checked concentration and purity by spectrophotometry.

Skills gained

Aseptic cell culture technique RNA isolation Spectrophotometry
RT-qPCR · Stop 2

RNA Cleanup, Reverse Transcription & Integrity Check

Removed contaminating genomic DNA, reverse-transcribed RNA into cDNA, and verified RNA integrity by gel electrophoresis.

Skills gained

gDNA removal Reverse transcription RNA integrity analysis
RT-qPCR · Stop 3

Primer Validation I

Ran gradient PCR to identify the optimal annealing temperature for reference-gene and target-gene primers.

Skills gained

Gradient PCR Primer optimization
RT-qPCR · Stop 4

Primer Validation II

Confirmed primer specificity by agarose gel electrophoresis and built a dilution series to calculate primer efficiency.

Skills gained

Gel electrophoresis Efficiency-curve construction
RT-qPCR · Stop 5

RT-qPCR Experiment

Ran the full qPCR experiment testing whether ascorbate-2-phosphate treatment changes Collagen I expression in RAT2 fibroblasts, analyzing results with the ΔΔCt method.

Skills gained

Quantitative PCR Relative gene expression analysis (ΔΔCt)

Where the two lines meet: both tracks ran on the same RAT2 fibroblast cell model — one asking a protein-level question, the other a transcript-level one.

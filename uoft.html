Research

Alongside coursework, I completed a research thesis (HMB496Y1) in the Department of Cell and Systems Biology, supervised by Jimmy Fraigne.

Role of Hypothalamic A11 Dopamine–Histamine Circuit in Controlling Wakefulness

The hypothalamic A11 region contains an unusually high proportion of wake-promoting dopamine cells, but its circuitry with the histamine-producing tuberomammillary nucleus (TMN) — a region also implicated in wakefulness — was unknown. Using in-vivo optogenetics and immunohistochemistry in mice, I investigated how the two regions interact. Activating the A11-to-TMN circuit prolonged wake episodes and directly activated TMN histamine cells, pointing to a previously uncharacterized dopamine–histamine circuit that helps sustain wakefulness.

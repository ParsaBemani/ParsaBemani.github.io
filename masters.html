Masters of Medical Sciences

About the program

This is a research-focused master's program, so most of my time is split between the bench and the questions that bring me to it. The year runs on several tracks at once: a wet-lab technique bootcamp building a working toolkit across protein and gene-expression methods, a journal club running alongside it, a year-long group capstone on Parkinson's diagnosis, a Friday seminar series, and coursework in communication, ethics and policy.

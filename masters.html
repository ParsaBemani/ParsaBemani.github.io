Masters of Medical Sciences

About the program

The MSc in Interdisciplinary Medical Sciences at Schulich Medicine & Dentistry is a one-year, course-based degree built around teams rather than a single thesis lab — its motto, Collaborate. Innovate. Adapt., describes the structure fairly literally. The program is based on the second floor of Western's Natural Science Centre, with bench work in the Drimmer laboratory.

Coursework runs in four-week blocks taken as a cohort: eight core courses spanning scientific communication, research design and analysis, science policy, ethical research practice, academic integrity, data science, diversity in research, and commercialization — plus two skill-development courses in interdisciplinary skills and career development. At the end of every block, students write a progress report that proposes and justifies their own final mark.

Alongside the coursework are three team rotations — basic science, clinical science, and community-engaged — all set within a single assigned field of study. That field is also the subject of the year-long capstone, where a team identifies a wicked problem within it, scopes it through an environmental scan, reviews the literature, and closes with a final report and a public showcase presentation. This ePortfolio is the program's other milestone, presented to the program director at the end of each term.

    CliftonStrengths Assessment

  • Dr. Sean Cain

    August 6, 2026

    Handling Difficult Conversations

    Dr. Cain, who is an emergency room physician, talked to us about what it is like to be in the ER as a physician and how to handle the pressure and strain of the environment. He took us through what it takes to be an expert at handling stressful situations, and the concept always came back to being able to handle difficult and hard conversations.

    As someone who deals with individuals in high distress every day, Dr. Cain explained the importance of having the skillset to speak to individuals in a manner that comforts them and makes them feel understood and heard. He noted that sometimes the easiest way to make someone who is stressed and under duress calm down is to make them feel heard and understood, even if you cannot provide immediate help to the individual.

    Dr. Cain also taught us the 5 key clinical practices that help foster better connection with patients.

    1. Familiarize yourself with the patient's condition, with the intent to help them
    2. Be a good listener
    3. Acknowledge the patient's views on what is causing them the most concern
    4. Make a connection with the patient's current situation
    5. Be aware of the patient's emotions

    These characteristics could also be translated into day-to-day interactions with individuals who are feeling down and stressed, and Dr. Cain's talk about these skills made me evaluate how I can improve these skills in myself to better understand and have difficult conversations and help people in stressful times.

  • Dr. Campbell & Dr. Wood

    June 12, 2026

    Microsoft Suite

    This seminar was given by Dr. Campbell and Dr. Wood, who took us through a master class on using the different tools at our disposal through Microsoft. This seminar stood out because it taught me new tools and ways to become more professional. I learned in this session that the little details are what can make me stand out among my peers. From generating a clean and formatted CV to drafting an email with a professional signature, I learned many new ways to become more professional by using every tool available to me.

    The session walked us through the different Microsoft tools and apps in detail, covering features in Word, Excel, Teams, Outlook and OneDrive that I hadn't used before, and showing how each one fits into a professional's day-to-day work. Seeing them demonstrated side by side made clear how much of the Microsoft suite goes unused day to day, and how much of it could be put toward staying on top of our professional game.

    From this session I took away the importance of having a structured workflow and how I can use tools such as Microsoft Teams, Outlook and OneDrive to organize my work and schedule to be on top of everything.

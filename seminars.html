  • Meg Kelly

    June 19, 2026

    CliftonStrengths Assessment

    The talk given by Meg Kelly was a great insight into how I perceived my strengths and weaknesses. This seminar made me rethink how I go about evaluating myself and made me focus on developing my top strengths instead of trying to eliminate my weaknesses. Through this session, I learned what my top strengths are and how I could best utilize them together to achieve progress.

    What I learned from this seminar has been guiding me to use my identified strengths as a tool to tailor my goals and approaches in a way that best fits my abilities.

    My Top 5 CliftonStrengths

    • 1. Achiever

      I work hard and have great stamina, taking real satisfaction in being busy and productive.

    • 2. Focus

      I set a direction, follow through, and make the corrections needed to stay on track.

    • 3. Restorative

      I am skilled at identifying what's wrong and figuring out how to fix it.

    • 4. Discipline

      I thrive on routine and structure, and bring order to the world around me.

    • 5. Analytical

      I search for reasons and causes, weighing all the factors that could affect a situation.

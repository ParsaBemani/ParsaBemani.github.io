Currently

I'm a graduate student in a research-focused master's program, where most of my time is split between the bench and the questions that bring me to it. Right now I'm working through a wet-lab technique bootcamp, building up a working toolkit across protein and gene-expression methods — western blotting on one track, RT-qPCR on the other. Alongside that, I'm part of a year-long capstone team studying Parkinson's diagnosis techniques. I'm also preparing for an upcoming community rotation, which will take that work outside the lab and into the community our project is meant to serve.

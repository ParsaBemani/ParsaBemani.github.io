Who am I?

Hello, my name is Parsa. I recently graduated from the University of Toronto and I am a graduate student in the Masters of Medical Sciences at the University of Western Ontario.

My time is split between academia and sports. I enjoy learning everything I can about the human body, and my passion is to understand how it works and to aspire to become a physician.

My aim in life is to promote the overall health of as many individuals as I can, through providing a better understanding of science-backed tools to improve performance and health.

Whenever I'm not attending a lecture or studying, I spend my time being physically active and further developing my passion in sports.

Here you can find more information about me and what I have accomplished so far and what I'm currently doing in my program.

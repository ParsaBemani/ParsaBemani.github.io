Upcoming Works

A community rotation that takes our capstone work out of the literature and into the community it's about.

The rotation

As part of my capstone team, I'll be completing a community rotation in partnership with Parkinson Society Southwestern Ontario (PSSO). It's the natural next step for a project about how Parkinson's is diagnosed — a chance to work alongside the organization that supports people living with the diagnosis.

About PSSO

Parkinson Society Southwestern Ontario is a registered charity based in London, Ontario. Its mission is to improve quality of life and care for people living with Parkinson's across Southwestern Ontario, and its work spans four areas:

Support services

Volunteer-led support groups for people with Parkinson's and their care partners.

Education

Professional education for healthcare providers, plus regional workshops.

Advocacy

Raising the profile of Parkinson's and the needs of the community it affects.

Research

Supporting research, and sharing developments through a bi-annual magazine.

What we'll be doing

Our group will run an outreach program to physicians on PSSO's behalf. The aim is to help connect physicians' Parkinson's patients with the programs and services PSSO already offers — closing the gap between a diagnosis in a clinic and the support available after it.

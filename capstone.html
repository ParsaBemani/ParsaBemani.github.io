Parkinson's Diagnosis Techniques

A capstone project examining how Parkinson's disease is diagnosed — and how that could be done better.

Overview

Parkinson's disease is still diagnosed largely on clinical presentation, often well after the underlying changes have begun. Over the course of this year, our capstone team is working through the techniques currently used to diagnose Parkinson's — where they hold up, where they fall short, and what newer approaches might offer. The goal is a clear-eyed evaluation of these diagnostic techniques and what would make diagnosis earlier or more reliable.

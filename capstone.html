Year-long group project

Parkinson's Diagnosis Techniques

Examining how Parkinson's disease is diagnosed and how it could be done better.

Overview

Parkinson's disease is still diagnosed largely on clinical presentation, often well after the underlying changes have begun. Over the course of this year, our capstone team is working through the techniques currently used to diagnose Parkinson's, and what newer approaches might offer. The goal is an evaluation of these diagnostic techniques and what would make diagnosis earlier or more reliable, in order to provide a more personalized approach to managing symptoms.

Research Question / Objective

Our work is framed around evaluating and improving the techniques used to diagnose Parkinson's disease, and to identify how effectively current Parkinson's disease symptom tracking methods capture individual disease progression.

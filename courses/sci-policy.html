Coursework

Science Policy

Taught by Dr. Croker.

About the course

This course covered how healthcare policies and regulations are developed and implemented through partnership between Health Canada and government bodies. The focus of the course was on understanding the structure and steps that Health Canada takes to implement strategies which aim to advance public health through using government levers in order to engage potential stakeholders.

Selected work

  1. 01

    Literature Review

    Increasing Access to Pediatric Medication

    What was done

    For the initial part of our policy development, our group developed a literature review of the current status of medications given to pediatric patients in Canada. The review looked at current limitations to pediatric drugs and their prescriptions and compared them to the current standard practices in European countries. Finally, we focused on the main factors contributing to the current lack in pediatric-specific medications and how the gap can be addressed.

    Why

    Through this assignment we generated the basis for further expanding our strategy towards our final presentation, which required a potential policy implementation to bridge the current gap seen in pediatric medication.

    What I learned

    This literature review further developed my understanding of the current dilemmas faced in pediatric healthcare and clinical trials. I also identified that in order to develop a strategy, it is essential to have good research backing to approach the problem we are trying to solve.

  2. 02

    Solutions Development

    Expanded Funding in Pediatric Clinical Trials for Drug Development

    What was done

    Within our policy teams, we developed a potential solution which could be implemented to bridge the gap in access to pediatric medication. The policy was developed based on the current challenges and limitations that were identified throughout the previous literature review.

    Why

    This assignment allowed us to brainstorm potential solutions to increase access to pediatric medication and to structure the steps needed to be taken in order to implement the solution. Before presenting the solution, more preparation had to be made in order to identify potential stakeholders and implement strategies to engage them with the posited solution and policies, and specific government levers had to be identified which could provide help to further our strategy within Health Canada's mandate.

    What I learned

    I had the opportunity to discuss multiple different solutions that could increase access to pediatric medication and brainstorm how different approaches would impact different government organizations and other stakeholders.

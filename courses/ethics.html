Coursework

Academic Integrity

About the course

Within this course, we learned about the importance of conducting ourselves within teams as well as mechanisms we could use to ensure effective teamwork is achieved. A large portion of lectures were focused on analyzing potentially unethical scenarios in groups and coming up with solutions to prevent further discord within the scenario. This was a theme that was a part of many lectures that had me learn how to approach different scenarios and how various approaches can be used to resolve conflict within teams and other ethical dilemmas.

Selected work

  1. 01

    Assignment

    Retraction Story Assignment

    What was done

    An article which had been retracted from academic journals was picked and analysed to understand the backstory on why it was retracted and the various reasons and author mistakes that led to its removal from academic journals.

    Why

    This assignment provided a better understanding of the common mistakes that can be made by authors in academia and how they can lead to the removal of a paper.

    What I learned

    From this assignment I learned the standards to which academic papers are held. Also, this assignment provided me with the opportunity to critically evaluate the potential reasons behind misconduct and plagiarism in academic material. Furthermore, I learned transferrable skills that can be taken into my future writing and publication practices to ensure a high professional standard.

    Referenced

    Lesné, S., Koh, M. T., Kotilinek, L., Kayed, R., Glabe, C. G., Yang, A., Gallagher, M., & Ashe, K. H. (2006). A specific amyloid-β protein assembly in the brain impairs memory [Retracted article]. Nature, 440, 352–357. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04533

    Lesné, S., Koh, M. T., Kotilinek, L. et al. Retraction Note: A specific amyloid-β protein assembly in the brain impairs memory. Nature, 631, 240 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07691-8

  2. 02

    Assignment

    AI Misconduct & Prevention

    What was done

    For this assignment, we had to develop a novel approach to how artificial intelligence (AI) can be used to commit unethical research practices in biomedical sciences. After the development of the approach we had to form a structure in order to safeguard against such unethical misconduct by suggesting various implementations which can promote integrity and research quality.

    Why

    By taking the view of an unethical researcher, we can develop a better understanding of the various ways AI is used to perform unethical acts and recognize potential red flags in research. We were also challenged to brainstorm how these acts can be subsequently prevented to promote ethical research practices.

    What I learned

    From this assignment I developed an understanding of how AI can be used for misconduct in research. However, this deeper understanding gave me the skillset to better recognize red flags in research and academic writing.

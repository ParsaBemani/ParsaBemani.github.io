Coursework

Scientific Communication

Taught by program chair Dr. Nicole Campbell and Dr. Adrian Owen.

About the course

This course built a practical toolkit for communicating science to a range of audiences — from peer reviewers to funding panels to the public. Three experiences stood out:

  1. 01

    Debate

    Open Access to Scientific Literature

    Arguing the against side, I built a case around the risks of unrestricted public access to research, sharpening my ability to construct a rigorous argument and back claims with credible evidence.

  2. 02

    Microteach assignment

    How credit cards work

    A short teaching pitch distilling a technical topic into a clear explanation for a general audience.

  3. 03

    SciComm outreach proposal

    Awakening Interest

    A funding proposal for a half-day outreach event introducing undergraduates to sleep research, designed to spark interest in research careers through talks and panels with current sleep scientists.

Across all three, the throughline was the same: effective communication starts with knowing your audience.
